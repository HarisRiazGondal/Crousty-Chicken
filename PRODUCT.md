# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in and around La Louvière who want to browse Crousty Chicken's menu, find the restaurant, call, or place an online order. Many visitors will use the site on a phone while deciding where or what to eat.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Crousty Chicken and make its menu, student offers, location, and ordering route easy to find. Success means visitors can quickly understand the food and take the next step to visit, call, or order.

## Brand Personality

Casual, local, affordable. The site should feel welcoming and energetic without implying an unverified price or offer condition.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic fast-food templates and copy that could describe any restaurant. Keep the real La Louvière identity, supplied logo, and tray-style food imagery central.

## Design Principles

- Make menu, directions, and ordering obvious, especially on small screens.
- Use the restaurant's own red and blue identity with its food imagery.
- Keep language direct, friendly, and grounded in confirmed business information.
- Treat student offers as an invitation to ask for current details unless terms are confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize readable text contrast, responsive layouts, keyboard navigation, and clear focus states. No additional specific accessibility requirements were provided.
